# S17.4.1 — Declare the asset subpaths in each library's `package.json`

Brief for
[S17.4.1](../F17.4-asset-exports.md#s1741-declare-the-asset-subpaths-in-each-librarys-packagejson),
at `0035837`.

## Goal

`ng-bootstrap` and `ng-tailwind` declare their asset subpaths in their source
`package.json` `exports`, which ng-packagr merges into the dist manifest, so the
post-build script `tools/scripts/patch-package-exports.mjs` and its two Nx
targets are deleted.

## Current behaviour

- `libs/ng-bootstrap/project.json:37-47` and
  `libs/ng-tailwind/project.json:37-47` define `patch-package-exports` and
  `"pack": { "dependsOn": ["patch-package-exports"] }`.
- `tools/scripts/patch-package-exports.mjs` adds the subpaths to
  `dist/<root>/package.json` after the build.
- Neither source `package.json` has an `exports` field.
- `sonar-project.properties:10` is generated by kiln from
  `.rn-forge/kiln/config.toml`.
