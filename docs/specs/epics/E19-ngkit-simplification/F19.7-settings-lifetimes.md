Keep settings persistence and notification in one owner while consumers release
their subscriptions. Coordinate with E5's auth-UI replacement before building;
apply this contract to the surviving settings owner rather than restoring an
outgoing service.

## S19.7.1 — Release settings observers with their views

**State:** Closed (2026-11-18)

**Acceptance:**

- The settings API exposes disposal or binds notification to a supplied
  lifetime. Color-mode and every other registered consumer release their
  observer at destruction; repeated mount/destroy does not multiply callbacks.
- Tests prove immediate current-value delivery and later updates for live
  consumers, and no delivery to destroyed consumers. Avoid replacing the small
  service with a new state-management framework.

Outcome: `UserSettingsService.registerChangeListener` returns a disposer
(additive); `rnf-color-mode` and `rnf-header` call it on destroy.

## S19.7.2 — Reset from fresh defaults and notify consumers

**State:** New

**Acceptance:**

- Clear, update, clear restores the declared defaults every time; exported
  defaults and other service instances remain unchanged.
- Clearing persists the empty override set and notifies live consumers of their
  effective reset values, so current widgets agree with storage.
- Missing stored values, unrelated custom keys and ordinary updates retain their
  documented behavior. E7's deferred theme-controller design is not introduced
  by this settings repair.

## Acceptance

- S19.7.1: Settings observers do not outlive their consumers.
- S19.7.2: Reset has fresh defaults and consistent stored/rendered state.
